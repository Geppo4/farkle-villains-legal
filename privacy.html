Privacy Policy — Farkle Villains

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Farkle Villains Android app ("the App", "we") handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Developer / data controller: Geppo (Farkle Villains).
Contact: privacygamegeppo@gmail.com

1. Information we handle

a) Game progress on your device. The App stores your progress on the device: level, experience points, coins, badges, dice, achievements, statistics, match history, the name you choose in the game and your settings (audio, vibration, language).

b) Google Play Games sign-in (optional). If you sign in with Google Play Games:

  • your game progress is saved to Google "Saved Games", so you can restore it on another device;
  • your scores (Score, Wins, Best streak) are sent to Google Play Games leaderboards and are publicly visible with your Play Games profile;
  • the App shows your Play Games profile name inside the game.

This data is processed by Google through Google Play Games. If you don't sign in, the game works locally only.

c) Advertising (Google AdMob). The App shows ads through Google AdMob: rewarded videos, which you choose to watch, and full-screen ads between matches. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process your device's advertising ID, general device information, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interaction data and diagnostic data. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent management platform (UMP). In these countries you can change your choice at any time in the App: Settings → Privacy & ads → Manage.

d) In-app purchases. The App offers optional purchases (ad removal and coin packs). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play: we do not receive or store payment details. To deliver and restore purchases, the App keeps the list of purchased products and the order IDs together with your game progress, on your device and, if you are signed in, in Google Play Games "Saved Games". For refunds and payment issues you can contact Google Play or write to us at the email above.

e) What we do NOT collect. We do not collect phone numbers, contacts, precise location, photos or files. We have no servers of our own: data only goes through the Google services listed above.

2. How we use information

  • To save and restore your game progress.
  • For Google Play Games leaderboards.
  • To show ads, which keep the App free.
  • To deliver and restore in-app purchases.
  • To make the App work properly and diagnose technical problems.

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Play Games, Google Play services and Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from partner sites and apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Advertising choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you can change your consent in the App (Settings → Privacy & ads → Manage). Everywhere, on Android you can also delete or reset your advertising ID from Settings → Google → Ads.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device: removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.
  • Google Play Games data (saved games and scores): you can delete it at any time. Instructions are on the Delete your data page.
  • For any request you can email us at the address above.

6. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, please contact us.

7. Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes

We may update this Policy. Changes will be shown by updating the date at the top of the page.
